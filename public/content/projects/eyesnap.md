# EyeSnap

> Diabetic retinopathy detection prototype — Best Use of MATLAB at HackHarvard 2023.

**Role:** ML / Full-stack · **Year:** 2023 · **Status:** Best Use of MATLAB — HackHarvard 2023

## The Problem
Diabetic retinopathy screening is expensive and slow, especially in low-resource settings. The equipment and specialists needed to catch it early simply aren't there for most of the people at risk.

## What I Built
A healthcare prototype that uses a **CNN** to detect diabetic retinopathy — **92% accuracy** on a 2,000-image validation set, trained on the NIH database (10,000+ images). It's a React Native app with **offline processing**, which brings diagnosis time from **~2 days → ~10 minutes**.

## Tech Stack
`Computer Vision` · `TensorFlow / CNN` · `React Native` · `Flask` · `SQL`
