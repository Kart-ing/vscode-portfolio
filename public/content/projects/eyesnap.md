# EyeSnap

> Diabetic retinopathy detection that cuts diagnostic cost ~99.8%.

**Role:** ML / Full-stack · **Year:** 2023 · **Status:** 2nd Place — HackHarvard (CareYaya Track)

## The Problem
Diabetic retinopathy screening is expensive and slow, especially in low-resource settings. The equipment and specialists needed to catch it early simply aren't there for most of the people at risk.

## What I Built
A healthcare app that uses a **CNN** to detect diabetic retinopathy — **92% accuracy** on a 2,000-image validation set, trained on the NIH database (10,000+ images). It's a React Native app with **offline processing**, which drops diagnostic setup cost from **~$50,000 → ~$100** and diagnosis time from **~2 days → ~10 minutes**.

## Tech Stack
`Computer Vision` · `TensorFlow / CNN` · `React Native` · `Flask` · `SQL`
