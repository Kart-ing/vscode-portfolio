# Motion-Sickness Reduction (Spectacles)

> Stabilization tech to make AR comfortable in motion.

**Role:** AR Developer · **Year:** 2025 · **Status:** Snap Spectacles Accelerator project

## The Problem
AR glasses induce motion sickness, especially in vehicles and transit. The mismatch between what you see and what your body feels makes extended AR use uncomfortable exactly where it could be most useful.

## What I Built
A stabilization approach that reduces motion sickness during AR use in moving environments, aimed at making extended wear comfortable. Built as part of the **Snap Spectacles Accelerator** work.

## Tech Stack
`Lens Studio` · `TypeScript` · `Real-time sensor / stabilization`

- Targets in-vehicle and transit use
