# Reality Rush

> AR fitness trainer — Snap AR Challenge winner and Best DEI Hack sponsored by Fidelity, HackPSU Fall 2024.

**Role:** Lead AR Developer · **Year:** 2024 · **Status:** Snap AR Challenge winner and Best DEI Hack sponsored by Fidelity, HackPSU Fall 2024

## The Problem
At-home workouts lack real-time form feedback. Without a coach watching, people drift into bad form, lose motivation, and risk injury — and most apps can only count reps, not correct them.

## What I Built
A **Snap Spectacles** AR fitness trainer. It uses Lens Studio's ML Kit for real-time pose tracking at **90% accuracy** with **sub-100ms feedback** for live form correction, and a gesture-based UI built on the Spectacles Hand Tracking API. It won the **Snap AR Challenge** and **Best DEI Hack sponsored by Fidelity** at HackPSU Fall 2024, among 200+ participants.

## Tech Stack
`Lens Studio` · `TypeScript` · `ML Kit (pose tracking)` · `Spectacles Hand Tracking API`
