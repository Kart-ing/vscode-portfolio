# Reality Rush

> AR fitness trainer — 1st place at HackPSU Snap AR ($5,000).

**Role:** Lead AR Developer · **Year:** 2024 · **Status:** 1st Place Winner

## The Problem
At-home workouts lack real-time form feedback. Without a coach watching, people drift into bad form, lose motivation, and risk injury — and most apps can only count reps, not correct them.

## What I Built
A **Snap Spectacles** AR fitness trainer. It uses Lens Studio's ML Kit for real-time pose tracking at **90% accuracy** with **sub-100ms feedback** for live form correction, and a gesture-based UI built on the Spectacles Hand Tracking API. It took 1st place out of 200+ participants at Snap's premier AR hackathon.

## Tech Stack
`Lens Studio` · `TypeScript` · `ML Kit (pose tracking)` · `Spectacles Hand Tracking API`

## Highlights
- 🥇 **1st place / $5,000** at HackPSU Snap AR (200+ participants)
- Real-time pose tracking at **90% accuracy**
